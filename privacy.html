Privacy Policy

Fold Count+ for iPhone

Support Privacy Policy 简体中文

In short: Fold Count+ does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. Your counts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Fold Count+ is developed by Fan Tian (“we”). If you have a question about this policy, write to fayne.tien@gmail.com.

What Fold Count+ stores, and where

  • Fold counts. For each day: how many times your iPhone was unfolded and folded, and the unfolds in each hour of the day.
  • Semi-folds. A semi-fold is your iPhone Duo held partly open for 3 seconds while Fold Count+ is on screen. For each day Fold Count+ stores how many there were, how many fell into each 5° range of hinge angle, and the sum of their angles (for the average). It also stores the time (hour and minute) and angle of each semi-fold, up to the latest 50 per day on each installation. The app shows the latest 20 of the last 30 days.
  • When records changed. For each day’s record, when it was started and last changed, and the time of each Reset All Counts, so that counts from before a reset stay deleted when they reach a device later.
  • A random installation identifier, used only to keep counts from different installations apart when they sync. It is not linked to you or your Apple Account.
  • Your settings, such as whether to count unfolds only or unfolds and folds, and the app’s language.

Fold Count+ does not record where you are, what you do on your iPhone, or any other content.

Without Fold Count+ Pro, counts and semi-folds are kept only in your iPhone’s memory while the app runs and are erased when it closes or the iPhone restarts. With Fold Count+ Pro, they are saved on your iPhone.

iCloud

With Fold Count+ Pro, everything listed above except the installation identifier and the language is synced through Apple’s iCloud to your private iCloud database, so it appears on your other devices and comes back after a reinstall. This includes the semi-fold angles and times. Only you can access this data; we cannot see it. iCloud is provided by Apple and covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

If iCloud sync can’t start, Fold Count+ says so and keeps your counts on this iPhone only until it can. You can stop syncing by turning off iCloud for Fold Count+ in the Settings app.

You can delete your counts at any time with Settings › Reset All Counts in Fold Count+. This deletes them on this iPhone and in iCloud; your other devices delete them when they next sync. You can also manage iCloud storage in the Settings app.

Hinge and automations

While Fold Count+ is open on iPhone Duo, it reads whether the iPhone is open, closed or partly open, and the hinge angle, to count unfolds, folds and semi-folds. An angle is recorded only for a semi-fold, as described above. Counting in the background uses Shortcuts automations that you create and that run on your iPhone; they only report that the iPhone was opened or closed. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Fold Count+ Pro is sold through the App Store, as a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details and no personal information about you. The app asks the App Store only whether Fold Count+ Pro is unlocked for your Apple Account or through Family Sharing, and when a subscription’s current period ends.

Data we collect

None. Fold Count+ has no servers, sends no data to us or to anyone else, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated App Store and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone’s settings.

Children

Fold Count+ collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
